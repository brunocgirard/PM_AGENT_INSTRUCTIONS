# ADKAR stages and adoption signals

- **Awareness** — employees know why the change is happening. Signal: % of target audience who can name the reason for the change (pulse survey item).
- **Desire** — employees want to support and participate. Signal: voluntary sign-up rate or opt-in to early-adopter cohort.
- **Knowledge** — employees know how to change (what to do and how). Signal: post-training assessment score or knowledge-check pass rate.
- **Ability** — employees can apply the new knowledge and skills on the job. Signal: observed competency or manager-confirmed proficiency rating within 30 days of training.
- **Reinforcement** — the change sticks and regression is prevented. Signal: sustained utilization rate at 60/90-day post-go-live check.
